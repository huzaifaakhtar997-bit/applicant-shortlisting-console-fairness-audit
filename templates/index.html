Written Justification of Metrics Beyond Accuracy (Stage E):

• Precision (Positive Predictive Value): In corporate shortlisting, recruiter and hiring manager interview bandwidth is tightly capped. False positives waste hundreds of expensive interviewer hours on unqualified candidates.

• Recall (True Positive Rate / Sensitivity): The opportunity cost of a false negative is severe: failing to shortlist a top data scientist allows competitor firms to hire them. High recall guarantees qualified applicants are not missed.
